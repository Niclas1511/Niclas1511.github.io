How to play

Hold jump for a high arc; release for a short hop. Press again for a double jump. Down ducks or speeds up your fall. Throw at your current height: duck for low targets, jump for high ones.

Successful throws build a combo up to ×5: a hit at ×3 earns 15 points instead of 5. A completely missed volley resets the combo; one hit is enough to save a triple volley. Close dodges earn 10 points and restore one chalk if there is room.

Choose a safe ground pickup or jump for the upper reward and dodge the obstacle behind it. Outlined power-ups give unlimited chalk (∞, 10s), triple throws (3, 10s), or invincibility (shield, 6s). Effects combine.

Watch the right edge for flying obstacles. Hit a student to answer their question. Worksheets fold into harmless paper planes. Each semester phase brings new patterns; Office hours and Inbox overflow are announced in advance.

Every 60 seconds of running, Reviewer 2 starts a boss arena. Scrolling stops: move with A/D or Left/Right, aim with the mouse and click to throw. On touchscreens hold the movement buttons and tap a target to aim and shoot. Keyboard only: W/S adjusts aim, X throws. Jump or move away from the announced attacks. Six chalk hits win the fight and earn 100 extra points. Chalk recharges every second in the arena. The run resumes only after victory; the next boss arrives after another 60 seconds of running.
