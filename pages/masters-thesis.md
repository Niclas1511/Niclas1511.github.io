## Summary

*Mixed binomial edge ideals*
{: .work-translation}

This thesis studies mixed binomial edge ideals, combining classical binomial edge ideals and parity binomial edge ideals in a common framework. It explores the relationship between the combinatorial properties of the underlying graphs and the algebraic properties of the ideals, with particular attention to Gröbner bases, saturation, and minimal prime ideals.
