## Master's thesis

### Gemischte Binomkantenideale

*Mixed binomial edge ideals* · 20 February 2025 · Written in German

This thesis studies mixed binomial edge ideals, combining classical binomial edge ideals and parity binomial edge ideals. It investigates their algebraic and combinatorial properties, including Gröbner bases, saturation, and minimal prime ideals.

Submitted at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, supervised by [Thomas Kahle](https://thomas-kahle.de/).
